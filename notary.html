Texas Statutory Notary Fee Schedule

TX Gov't Code §406.024

Pursuant to Texas Government Code §406.024 (as updated by the 88th Texas Legislature), the maximum statutory fees for notarial acts in the State of Texas are as follows:

Notarial Act (Texas Government Code §406.024) Statutory Fee
Taking the acknowledgment or proof of any deed or other instrument in writing, for registration (includes certificate & seal, first signature) $10.00
For each additional signature on an acknowledgment or proof $1.00
Administering an oath or affirmation with certificate and seal (Jurat) $10.00
Swearing a witness to a deposition, certificate, seal, and other business connected with taking the deposition $10.00
Certified copy of a record or paper in the notary public's office (non-recordable documents) $10.00
Mobile Travel, Printing & Loan Signing Package Fees: Under Texas Secretary of State rules, mobile travel, mileage, after-hours dispatch, dual-tray laser document printing, and scanback administrative services are separate from statutory notarial act fees and are quoted upfront and agreed upon prior to dispatch.
